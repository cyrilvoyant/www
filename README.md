# www.cyrilvoyant.com

Personal research website of **Cyril Voyant**, physicist and Research Director at Mines Paris – PSL (Centre O.I.E.).

*Understand before you compute.* People and their question at the centre, a model we understand at the core, every tool — artificial intelligence included — around it.

- English pages at the root, French pages under `/fr/` (plus the three historical French pages `/prevision-*`).
- The research stories are classified by step of the method: compute directly, hybridise, learn frugally, AI in its place, demonstrate trust.
- Technical guides, theory and runnable code: <https://cyrilvoyant.github.io/>. Author profile: <https://person.cyrilvoyant.com/>.

This repository holds the built static site (HTML, CSS, images), served by GitHub Pages. Pages are generated from the editorial sources of the author; please report errors through an issue.
