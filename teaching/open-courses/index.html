Open courses in French: biostatistics, medical physics, industrial physics and food heat transfer

Courses I taught to first-year health students and to food-science technicians, published in full: every slide with its text, one at a time, the decks and handouts as PDF, exercise sessions with their corrections. They are in French; the slide viewer works for everyone, and the formulas speak for themselves.

First-year health studies (PASS, UE8)

Biostatistics for health studies

Probabilities and Bayesian reasoning, diagnostic tests (sensitivity, specificity, ROC curves), descriptive statistics, the normal distribution and hypothesis tests, correlation, linear regression and χ² tests, with medical examples.

  • Probabilities, conditional probabilities, contingency tables and ROC curves
  • Descriptive statistics, the normal distribution and hypothesis tests
  • Correlation, linear regression and χ² tests

First-year health studies (PASS-PACES), physical bases of medical imaging

Physics for health studies: electromagnetic waves, X-rays and imaging

The nature of electromagnetic waves and of the photon, the energy levels of the atom, absorption, emission and spectroscopy (up to pulse oximetry), the production of X-rays, their interactions with matter and attenuation, then radiography and CT scanning; two corrected exercise sessions.

  • Course: electromagnetic waves and the physical bases of imaging
  • Exercise session 1, corrected
  • Exercise session 2, corrected

University technology degree (B.U.T.) in biological engineering, food science and biotechnology, 2nd year

Industrial physics: thermodynamics, water vapor and refrigeration

Quantities and changes of state, ideal and real gases, Clapeyron, entropy and Mollier diagrams, water vapor and humidity, engine cycles, then the refrigeration cycle, its coefficient of performance and its refrigerants; with the handouts.

  • General notions of thermodynamics
  • Ideal gases, real gases and diagrams
  • Water vapor
  • Thermodynamic engine cycles
  • Refrigeration cycles

University technology degree (B.U.T.) in biological engineering, food science and biotechnology, 1st year

Heat transfer for food engineering: cooling and freezing

Conduction, convection and the Biot number, thermal properties of foods, refrigeration power and the amount of water to freeze, then the energy balance of cooling or freezing a product.

  • Lecture: heat transfer, cooling and freezing of foods
