Open courses in French: biostatistics, medical physics, industrial physics and food heat transfer

Courses taught to first-year health students and to food-science technicians, listed chapter by chapter so that you can go straight to the part you need.

In brief

  • Four courses taught in French, published in full: every slide with its text, the decks and handouts as PDF, exercise sessions with their corrections. Biostatistics also exists in English; the interactive tools listed under each course are in English.
  • This page is the chapter index, in English. Each course is presented, with its goal, its prerequisites and a worked example, on the Teaching page; in French, on Enseignement.
  • License CC BY 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution): free reuse, including modification and commercial use, with credit to the author. For teaching only, not clinical recommendations.

Biostatistics for health studies

First-year health studies: PASS (parcours d'accès spécifique santé, the French first-year health program), teaching unit UE8

Probabilities and Bayesian reasoning, diagnostic tests (sensitivity, specificity, ROC curves: receiver operating characteristic, the share of sick people detected against the share of healthy people wrongly flagged as the threshold varies), descriptive statistics, the normal distribution and hypothesis tests, correlation, linear regression and χ² tests, with medical examples.

  1. Probabilities, conditional probabilities, contingency tables and ROC curves
  2. Descriptive statistics, the normal distribution and hypothesis tests
  3. Correlation, linear regression and χ² tests

Try it: ROC curve · Normal distribution · Student t-test · Influential point in regression · Significance and R² · χ² test · Regression intervals

Open the course · Goal, prerequisites and an example: the course on the Teaching page

Physics for health studies: electromagnetic waves, X-rays and imaging

First-year health studies (PASS, the French first-year health program): physical bases of medical imaging

The nature of electromagnetic waves and of the photon, the energy levels of the atom, absorption, emission and spectroscopy (up to pulse oximetry), the production of X-rays, their interactions with matter and attenuation, then radiography and computed tomography (CT) scanning; two corrected exercise sessions.

  1. Course: electromagnetic waves and the physical bases of imaging
  2. Exercise session 1, corrected
  3. Exercise session 2, corrected

Try it: Electromagnetic wave · Attenuation and half-value layer · Compton scattering

Open the course (in French) · Goal, prerequisites and an example: the course on the Teaching page

Industrial physics: thermodynamics, water vapor and refrigeration

University technology degree (B.U.T.) in biological engineering, food science and biotechnology, 2nd year

Quantities and changes of state, ideal and real gases, Clapeyron, entropy and Mollier diagrams, water vapor and humidity, engine cycles, then the refrigeration cycle, its coefficient of performance and its refrigerants; with the handouts.

  1. General notions of thermodynamics
  2. Ideal gases, real gases and diagrams
  3. Water vapor
  4. Thermodynamic engine cycles
  5. Refrigeration cycles

Try it: Heat engine and Carnot · Refrigeration, COP and Carnot · Freezing: energy and power

Open the course (in French) · Goal, prerequisites and an example: the course on the Teaching page

Heat transfer for food engineering: cooling and freezing

University technology degree (B.U.T.) in biological engineering, food science and biotechnology, 1st year

Conduction, convection and the Biot number, thermal properties of foods, refrigeration power and the amount of water to freeze, then the energy balance of cooling or freezing a product.

  1. Lecture: heat transfer, cooling and freezing of foods

Try it: Cooling and the Biot number · Freezing: energy and power
