Interactive tools: choose a question, explore a method

Calculators, teaching experiments and research applications, organized by domain and language.

Start with an example, change an input and examine what changes. Research calculators and medical applications are for research and education; their assumptions and limitations are stated in the linked pages.

Languages: the seven technical calculators have French and English interfaces. The course tools (ROC, normal distribution, regression, attenuation, cooling, freezing, COP) are in French. The forecast comparison exists in French and English; dedicated applications retain their existing interfaces; filter by language before opening a tool.
