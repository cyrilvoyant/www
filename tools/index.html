Scientific simulators, animations and calculators

Scientific simulators, interactive animations, and course or research calculators, organized by field and interface language.

Choose a scientific simulator, interactive animation or calculator. Change an input and examine what changes. Research calculators and medical applications are for research and education; their assumptions and limitations are stated in the linked pages.

Languages: the seven technical calculators have French and English interfaces. The course tools (ROC, normal distribution, regression, attenuation, cooling, freezing, COP) are in French. The forecast comparison exists in French and English; dedicated applications retain their existing interfaces; filter by language before opening a tool.
