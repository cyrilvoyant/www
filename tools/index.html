Languages: most tools work in French and English. Three applications are in English only. Courses and slides are in French.

PV forecasting modes

Compare a forecast with persistence, NICE

NICE divides a forecast’s error by that of persistence: 1 means “as good as persistence”, 0 a perfect forecast. Paste observations and forecasts, choose a horizon, read the result.

Measure variability and forecastability, sCV and F

sCV describes the variability of a signal, F its forecastability, before any model. Supply measured and clear-sky irradiance, then read them at your chosen horizon.

Displace a forecast and explore the γ-index

The γ-index judges a forecast with tolerances in space, time and intensity. Displace, delay or bias a synthetic cloud and adjust these tolerances.

Browser calculator · French and English

Explore ARTU coefficients and lookup table

ARTU is a reference forecast built on the autocorrelation of the signal (how much it resembles itself h and 2h earlier, h being the horizon). Vary these autocorrelations and measurement quality; read coefficients and weights.
