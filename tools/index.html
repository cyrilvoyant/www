PV forecasting modes

Forecast photovoltaic power directly, or through irradiance (GTI, GHI) then conversion: error of each stage, live nRMSE and NICE, peak power and curtailment, on the open SOLETE data.

Probabilistic solar forecasting

Hourly solar irradiance at Ajaccio: confidence vs prediction interval, why ±2σ fails, leakage-free conformal prediction, live PICP and CRPS.

Compare a forecast with persistence, NICE

NICE divides a forecast’s error by that of persistence: 1 means “as good as persistence”, 0 a perfect forecast. Paste observations and forecasts, choose a horizon, read the result.

Measure variability and forecastability, sCV and F

Displace a forecast and explore the γ-index

The γ-index judges a forecast with tolerances in space, time and intensity. Displace, delay or bias a synthetic cloud and adjust these tolerances.

Explore ARTU coefficients and lookup table

ARTU is a reference forecast built on the autocorrelation of the signal (how much it resembles itself h and 2h earlier, h being the horizon). Vary these autocorrelations and measurement quality; read coefficients and weights.

Compare forecasts: persistence, AR and ELM against horizon

Five models on public Palaiseau data, 30 min to 6 h: error against horizon, NICE, curves; computed by the PV_ELM_pred code. AR: autoregression, a regression on past values. ELM: a frugal neural network.

How to evaluate a forecast

Browser tool · English

FrugalCast: does your forecast beat persistence?

Paste your own time series and compare 30 frugal models (persistences, CLIPER, exponential smoothing, ARTU, Holt-Winters, Theta, AR, ARMA, periodic AR, 14 extreme learning machines) with prediction intervals and 20 metrics, including NICE and CRPS. Computed in your browser: the data never leave your computer.
