Regression: confidence interval and prediction interval

With 20 points and a residual standard deviation of 1 (the spread of the points around the line), the predicted mean at the center is known to ±0.47 (confidence interval), but a new observation only to ±2.15 (prediction interval). You know which interval answers which question.

A course experiment whose values are computed within the stated model and are not research findings: The chapter: summary, formulas and FAQ (slides in French) · All experiments · Glossary: prediction interval.

Testing the line

Before building an interval around the line, check that the slope is significant: the Student t test on the slope, with an extreme point, and r, R² and centring the data.

Try it and interpret

At the center of the scatter, compare the half-widths (distance from the center to the edge of the band), 0.47 and 2.15. Increase n (the number of points): the band for the mean narrows clearly, the band for an observation much less. Move x₀ (the x value where you predict) beyond the observed values: both widen and the extrapolation warning appears.

Why is the interval for a new observation wider than the interval for the mean?

The confidence interval concerns the mean of y at x₀: it contains only the uncertainty of the line and narrows as n increases. The prediction interval adds the spread of an individual observation, which does not vanish, even with a lot of data.

Show the formulas

y^0±tse1n+(x0−x¯)2Sxx

Confidence interval of the mean. For the prediction interval of a new observation, add 1 under the root.

Where: ŷ₀ is the value predicted by the line at x₀, t the Student quantile with n − 2 degrees of freedom for 95%, sₑ the residual standard deviation (spread of points around the line), n the number of points, x̄ the mean of x and Sxx = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)².

Definitions

Confidence interval
It brackets the mean of y at x₀.
Prediction interval
It brackets a new observation at x₀: add 1 under the root.
x₀, ŷ₀
The value where the prediction is made and the mean predicted by the line.
x̄, n, Sxx
The mean of the observed x, their number and the sum of squared deviations x − x̄.
sₑ (se)
The estimated residual standard deviation: the spread of points around the line.
t
The Student quantile for a 95% band, with n − 2 degrees of freedom. See the Student test.

Assumptions and limitations

Simple regression, independent normal errors of constant variance, constructed scatter. Bands hold at 95% at each x, not over the whole curve at once. In extrapolation, the model may be wrong without the band showing it.
