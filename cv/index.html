Curriculum vitae

Cyril Voyant
Physicist · Research Director, Mines Paris–PSL

Centre Observation, Impacts, Énergie (O.I.E.), Sophia Antipolis, France.
Visiting Professor, Faculty of Engineering, University of Kragujevac, Serbia.

I develop methods for solar and photovoltaic forecasting, time-series modeling and forecast evaluation. My background in medical physics connects these questions to measurement, uncertainty and decisions in practice.

cyril.voyant@minesparis.psl.eu · ORCID · HAL · Detailed academic CV

Current appointments and responsibilities

  • Since 2024, Research Director (DR2), Mines Paris–PSL, Centre O.I.E.
  • 2026, Visiting Professor, Faculty of Engineering, University of Kragujevac, Energy and Process Engineering.
  • Since 2025, Scientific coordinator, Mines Carnot ELECTRE programme, for AI applied to energy, forecasting and multi-source systems.
  • Since 2026, Internal Steering Committee member, The Transition Institute 1.5, Mines Paris–PSL.
  • Since 2026, Working Group Expert, ETIP PV, Digital PV, Grid and Storage.

Career

2015–2018 and 2019–2024, Part-time academic appointments, University of Corsica, SPE / UMR CNRS 6134. Research and teaching in physics, energy and numerical methods.

2018–2019, Associate Researcher, PIMENT laboratory, University of La Réunion.
2013–2015, Visiting Researcher, University of Corsica, SPE / UMR CNRS 6134.

2003–2024, Medical physics, alongside my research. Medical physicist at Polyclinique Saint-Roch, Montpellier (2003–2006), then CHD Castelluccio, Ajaccio (2006–2024). Head of the Medical Physics and Radiation Protection Unit in Ajaccio (2020–2024), with responsibilities in radiotherapy, quality assurance and commissioning.

Education

  • 2019, Habilitation à diriger des recherches (HDR), University of Corsica. Numerical simulation, from solar irradiation forecasting to life sciences.
  • 2011, PhD, University of Corsica. Time series and global solar radiation.
  • 2003, Medical physics qualification (DQPRM), INSTN, Saclay; radiation protection qualification (PCR), Institut Gustave Roussy.
  • 2002, DEA, Radiation and Medical Imaging, University of Toulouse.
  • 2001, Master's degree, Fundamental Physics, University of Montpellier II.

Teaching and research supervision

I supervise and co-supervise doctoral research on solar forecasting, satellite imagery, parsimonious models, energy-system control and radioiodine modeling. Previous doctoral work includes solar forecasting, air quality and solar-potential estimation.

At Mines Paris–PSL, my teaching includes solar resource forecasting and academic tutoring of international internships and gap years (UE45). Earlier teaching spans biostatistics, thermodynamics, biophysics and numerical modeling at the University of Corsica.

Scientific service

Associate Editor, Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio); Editorial Board Member, Sustainability, Energy Sustainability section. Editorial roles also include the Turkish Journal of Forecasting and the Journal of Radiology and Oncology.

Research-proposal reviewer for the Swiss National Science Foundation, Swiss Cancer Research and the Kuwait Foundation for the Advancement of Sciences; manuscript reviewer for international journals in energy, forecasting and medical physics.

Selected distinctions

  • Stanford / Elsevier Top 2% Scientists list, since 2020.
  • PVSEC Award, photovoltaic systems category, Hamburg, 2009.
  • City of Nice Prize, Accademia Corsa, 2012.
