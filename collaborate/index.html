Collaborate: research projects, studies and adaptation of the tools

Your question, a method you can check, a result you can defend.

Everything on these sites is open: the theory, the code, the limits. The next step is your data and your decision. Bring a real problem; we start from what is known about the system, beat an explicit reference, and add complexity only where it pays.

Three ways to work together

1

Answer a call for projects

National (ANR, ADEME), European (Horizon Europe) or regional calls, as partner or coordinator: energy forecasting, renewables on the grid, evaluation of models, medical physics, responsible AI in research.

2

Fund it directly with the school

A research contract with Mines Paris – PSL: a study, a tool adapted to your data, a co-supervised doctorate (for example under the French CIFRE scheme) or a postdoctoral project.

3

Do research together

An academic collaboration when the question interests both sides, with a valorization objective set from day one: a joint paper, open software, a dataset, a transfer to practice.

What can be adapted to your data

  • Forecast evaluation: NICE metrics, γ-index for forecast fields.
  • Reference forecasts: BLEND and CLIPER, ARTU, variability and forecastability with sCV and F.
  • Frugal learning: MIMO-ELM for an energy mix, satellite irradiance forecasting.
  • Medical physics: LQL-Equiv for dose equivalence, the radioiodine therapy model.
  • Simulation and AI in practice: road tunnel loads, WebSemantic (a language model reads, validated code computes).

How it works

  1. Write a short email: the question, the data, the decision it serves, the timeline.
  2. A first exchange frames the problem: what is known about the system, which reference to beat, what precision is really needed.
  3. The framework is chosen with the services of the school: call, contract or collaboration. Intellectual property, confidentiality and publication are set there.
  4. Results are judged against an explicit reference and, wherever the agreement allows, published with their code.
