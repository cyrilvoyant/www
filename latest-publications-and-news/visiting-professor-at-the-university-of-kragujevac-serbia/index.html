Visiting Professor at the University of Kragujevac, Serbia

Cyril Voyant

I am honoured to have been appointed Visiting Professor at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Kragujevac, together with my colleague and friend Prof. Gilles Notton.

This appointment formalises a long-standing and productive cooperation between Mines Paris-PSL, the University of Corsica, and the University of Kragujevac (Prof. Milan Despotovic). This collaboration has developed over several years through joint research, academic exchanges, Erasmus activities, and European project initiatives.

Scientific and practical interest

Our common wor focuses on renewable energy systems, solar irradiance forecasting, numerical modeling, data qualification, and AI-based methods designed for operational constraints. The collaboration is particularly valuable because it connects complementary expertise, different territorial contexts, and practical energy challenges across Europe.

Key message

Beyond the personal recognition, this appointment strengthens an active scientific and educational partnership between France, Corsica, and Serbia. It opens new perspectives for joint publications, student mobility, teaching activities, and future European projects in the field of energy transition.
