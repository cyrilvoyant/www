Appointed Associate Editor for Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio)

Cyril Voyant

Appointed Associate Editor for Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio), a multidisciplinary peer-reviewed journal with strong impact.

Handling submissions in my areas of expertise:

  • Renewable energy: Solar irradiance forecasting (GHI), ML applied to PV/hybridation, microgrids
  • Medical physics: Radiotherapy , radiobiology, equivalent dosimetry

This role aligns with my trajectory as Research Director at Mines Paris–PSL O.I.E. (since 2024), with >130 peer-reviewed publications (h-index ~35, >7000 citations, Stanford Top 2%). Scientific Reports covers all scientific domains.

The editorial team comprises >2000 global experts (https://www.nature.com/srep/about/editors). My appointment highlights French interdisciplinary research in energy/ML/medicine. .
