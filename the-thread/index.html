The thread: five steps, one way of working

Every piece of research starts with the same question: what do we already know? Depending on the answer, compute, complete a model or learn from data; then make the tool usable and demonstrate that it works. Follow the thread down: each step leads to its stories, each on its own page.

  • 1. Compute directly
  • 2. Hybridize
  • 3. Learn frugally
  • 4. AI in its place
  • 5. Demonstrate trust
1

Step 1 of 5 · Is the link between causes and effects known?

Compute directly

Yes: compute it, with an explicit model.

When the link is known, compute it with an explicit model, calibrated and validated on data; learning is added only for a demonstrated benefit.

Step 3 of 5 · Are the useful relations known?

Learn frugally

Not well enough: learn from data, with only the complexity that pays.

When the useful relations are not known well enough, evaluate what the data can teach, with only as much complexity as pays.
