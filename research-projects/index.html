Research and projects

My research at the Observation, Impacts, Energy center of Mines Paris – PSL, in Sophia Antipolis. center of Mines Paris – PSL asks three questions: what is predictable, how much model is needed, and how to judge a forecast for its intended use. It relies on collective programs, shown here with their dates and partners.

In brief

  • Three strands. Solar variability and forecastability (sCV, F); parsimonious forecasting (persistence, BLEND, ELM); evaluation that matches the question (NICE, γ-index).
  • Applications. Island energy systems, storage and microgrids; in medical physics, dose equivalence in radiotherapy.
  • Programs. Three current ones (Fine4Cast, scientific coordination of Flagship project of the Carnot M.I.N.E.S institute on the electrification of uses (2023-2026): materials, storage, industrial processes, relevance and limits of electrification., an agreement with the University of Corsica and the Center national de la recherche scientifique, the main French public research organization.) and four completed since 2015, on a timeline by domain.
  • Checkable evidence. Each strand links to the published paper, the story that explains it and the open code.

Solar variability and forecastability

A strong daily cycle can hide the variability caused by clouds. The stochastic coefficient of variation (sCV) separates the two: it measures cloud-driven variability once the daily cycle is set aside. The forecastability index F relates this variability to the forecast error one can expect. The published evaluation covers 68 Spanish stations.

The sCV and forecastability story · Which forecast for which signal? · method and MATLAB code · Renewable Energy paper

Parsimonious forecasting

I study how the structure of a signal and the complexity of a model shape forecast quality: autoregressive models (the forecast is a weighted sum of past values), Extreme Learning Machines (ELM: one-hidden-layer neural networks whose only computed weights are the output weights), clear-sky preprocessing (the theoretical irradiance without clouds) and cyclostationary persistence. Persistence, the reference forecast, repeats the last observed value; a cyclostationary series has statistical properties that repeat periodically, here every day. BLEND uses this structure to improve on persistence without training a model; the separate MIMO-ELM study (multiple inputs, multiple outputs) forecasts seven energy signals jointly.

Evaluation that matches the question

NICE metrics divide a model's errors by those of persistence, for several error norms. The γ-index (gamma) judges agreement between forecast and observation within explicit tolerances in space, time and intensity. Both complement the usual metrics; how to read them depends on the baseline, the sampling and the tolerances.

Energy systems and medical physics

Forecasts are used to run photovoltaic systems, storage and microgrids (small local electricity networks). Our projects connect forecasting to energy management and control, especially in island systems. In medical physics, I work on radiobiological dose equivalence, radiotherapy modeling and radioiodine kinetics.

Research programs and collaborations

I contribute to collective programs on forecasting, renewable integration and energy management. The timeline places them in time and by domain; the cards give the funder, the partners and the official record when one exists. Hover, tap or focus a dotted acronym for its definition.

Current programs

  • Fine4Cast

    Agence nationale de la recherche: the French public agency that funds research through competitive calls. / Priority research program (PEPR) on advanced technologies for energy systems (TASE), funded by France 2030 and operated by the ANR; it supports upstream research (readiness levels 1 to 4). · 2023–2028

    Forecasting renewable production and demand, from minutes to days ahead, at fine geographical and temporal resolution (full title: Next Generation Energy Demand and Renewable Production Forecasting Tools for Fine Geographical and Temporal Scales).

    Project overview · PEPR TASE on the ANR website

  • ELECTRE

    Carnot institute “Innovative methods for companies and society”: partnership research of the Écoles des Mines, ENSTA Paris and École polytechnique, holding the Carnot label since 2006; the ARMINES association manages the contracts. institute · projects 2023–2026 · scientific coordination since 2025

    Electrification of uses: materials, storage, industrial processes, relevance and limits of electrification. I coordinate the components on AI for energy, forecasting and multi-source systems.

    Project page (Carnot M.I.N.E.S institute, in French) · closing workshop, 18 November 2026

  • Mines Paris – PSL, University of Corsica, CNRS agreement

    Center national de la recherche scientifique, the main French public research organization. · 2024–2027

    Forecasting intermittent solar power and energy-management algorithms for smart microgrids.

Completed programs

  • SAPHIR

    Agence nationale de la recherche: the French public agency that funds research through competitive calls. · January 2022, initial duration 48 months

    High-resolution weather forecasts improved by sensor data, combining observations and numerical modeling. Coordinated by the Sciences for the Environment laboratory, University of Corsica and CNRS. laboratory, with the Laboratoire d'aérologie and Inria.

    ANR project record ANR-21-CE04-0014

  • HyLES

    Agence nationale de la recherche: the French public agency that funds research through competitive calls. · 2021–2024 (42 months)

    Hydrogen integration in weakly or non-interconnected grids: Corsica, La Réunion, French Polynesia. With Franche-Comté Electronics, Mechanics, Thermal Science and Optics – Sciences and Technologies institute (Besançon), coordinator of HyLES., Laboratory of energetics, electronics and processes, University of La Réunion., University of French Polynesia, a HyLES partner through its GEPASUD laboratory (South Pacific geosciences). and Università di Corsica Pasquale Paoli, Sciences for the Environment laboratory (SPE), a joint unit with the CNRS..

    ANR project record ANR-20-CE05-0035

  • INTELPOLY

    European Cooperation in Science and Technology: a European program that funds networks of researchers (the “Actions”), not the research itself. Action · 2021–2025

    Diagnosis, control and cybersecurity of polygeneration plants (combined production of electricity, heat or cooling) and hybrid storage.

  • TILOS

    The European Union framework program for research and innovation, 2014-2020. · February 2015 – January 2019

    Technology Innovation for the Local Scale, Optimum Integration of Battery Energy Storage: battery storage and renewable generation integrated on the Greek island of Tilos, a demonstrator followed by other islands including Corsica.

    Project record on Community Research and Development Information Service: the official database of EU-funded projects.

Platforms and other collaborations

  • University of Corsica research platform near Ajaccio that couples a photovoltaic field with hydrogen storage, connected to the island grid.: short-term solar forecasting for hydrogen storage and coupled production–demand management.
  • RESINTER / PAGLIA-ORBA: predictive control of a renewable electricity network.
  • ISERBATURB: integration of renewable systems into buildings and urban environments.
  • Franco-Polish cooperation: exchanges on renewable integration in island microgrids.
  • Technical University of Sofia / The European Union program for education, training and the mobility of students and staff.: co-design of a renewable-energy summer school.
