At a glance: research, software, courses and tools

The public work of Cyril Voyant in medical physics and energy forecasting, courses and software included, each item linked to its source: paper, DOI, repository, team. Free to reuse, crediting the authors.

Main ideas

Understand before you computeStart from the human question, model the system, then choose the tool.Beat a simple referenceEvery forecast is first compared with persistence; a model that does not beat it adds nothing.Complexity must payA frugal model (ELM, autoregression) first; heavier only if the gain is shown.Trust is demonstratedPublic code, data, licences and verification; AI is a tool, declared.

Research lines

  • Solar and energy forecastingPersistence, BLEND, ELM, dimensionality reduction, the Corsican electricity mix.
  • Evaluating forecastsNICE, γ-index, sCV and forecastability, prediction intervals.
  • Medical physicsBiologically equivalent doses (LQL-Equiv), radioiodine therapy of the thyroid.
  • AI and languageLanguage models as interfaces to validated codes.
  • Assessing researchInfluence index, the RTVACOS framework, scientific publishing.
