Cite and reuse our work

Everything published here may be reused. The only request: credit the authors and their laboratory, the O.I.E. center (Observation, Impacts, Energy) of Mines Paris – PSL, and link to the source.

WorkLicenseHow to cite
ArticlesThe journal's (often CC BY when open access)The full reference with every co-author and the DOI; the list is on the news page, ORCID and HAL.
SoftwareThe repository's (LICENSE file: MIT, BSD, GPL…)The repository's "all versions" Zenodo DOI, or GitHub's "Cite this repository" button (CITATION.cff), plus the associated article when there is one.
Courses (solar forecasting, biostatistics, industrial physics)CC BY 4.0 (lectures and exercise sessions)Voyant, C. (year). Course title. Mines Paris – PSL, O.I.E. center. Page address.
DataThe dataset's (for example CC BY on Webservice-Energy)The dataset DOI and version, with its producers.
Texts and figures of this site (stories, approach, AI page, data page)CC BY 4.0Voyant, C. (2026). Page title. www.cyrilvoyant.com, page address, date accessed.

Team work

Much of this work is collective: cite every author in the order of the publication. For the laboratory's infrastructures, such as Webservice-Energy (Lionel Ménard (ORCID), Raphaël Jolivet), cite the infrastructure, its authors and the O.I.E. center.

Logos, trademarks and third-party content shown on some pages keep their own rights. A question: write.
