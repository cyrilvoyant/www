Cite and reuse our work

Everything published here may be reused. The only request: credit the authors and their laboratory, the O.I.E. center (Observation, Impacts, Energy) of Mines Paris – PSL, and link to the source.

WorkLicenseHow to cite
ArticlesThe journal's (often CC BY when open access: free reuse, crediting the authors)The full reference with every co-author and the DOI (permanent identifier of the paper); the list is on the news page, with a BibTeX, RIS and CSL-JSON file for each paper (import formats of reference managers), ORCID (unique researcher identifier) and HAL (open archive).
SoftwareThe repository's (LICENSE file: MIT, BSD, GPL… are free-software licenses)The repository's "all versions" Zenodo DOI (Zenodo is the open archive that issues it), or GitHub's "Cite this repository" button (CITATION.cff, which says how to cite the repository), plus the associated article when there is one. All software, with its DOI.
Courses (the five open courses)CC BY 4.0 (lectures and exercise sessions)Voyant, C. (year). Course title. Mines Paris – PSL, O.I.E. center. Page address.
DataThe dataset's (for example CC BY on Webservice-Energy)The dataset DOI and version, with its producers.
Texts and figures of this site (stories, approach, AI page, data page)CC BY 4.0Voyant, C. (2026). Page title. www.cyrilvoyant.com, page address, date accessed.

Team work

Much of this work is collective: cite every author in the order of the publication. For the laboratory's infrastructures, such as Webservice-Energy (Lionel Ménard (ORCID), Raphaël Jolivet), cite the infrastructure, its authors and the O.I.E. center.
