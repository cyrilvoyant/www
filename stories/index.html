Research: questions, findings and methods

The thread connecting my research

My work connects medical physics and energy forecasting around one question: how can a model help inform a real decision? I start from the physical system, existing knowledge and available data. When the relation is known, I compute it; when it is incomplete, I complement the model; when learning is needed, I measure what its complexity adds. AI is a tool in this approach. Decisions, evaluation and responsibility remain human.
