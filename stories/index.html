Research: questions, findings and methods

The thread connecting this research

This work connects medical physics and energy forecasting around one question: how can a model help inform a real decision? The team starts from the physical system, existing knowledge and available data. When the relation is known, we compute it; when it is incomplete, we complement the model; when learning is needed, we measure what its complexity adds. AI is a tool in this approach. Decisions, evaluation and responsibility remain human.

Medical physics

Compute directly · Medicine

Atmospheric neutrons and BNCT: quantify the limit

Boron neutron capture therapy (BNCT), exploratory 2011 model: rare captures, local energy and a mean dose too low for macroscopic therapy.

Compute directly · Medicine

Compute, don't learn: radiobiological dose equivalence

A single 15 Gy fraction (Gy: gray, the unit of absorbed dose) to the rectum has a biologically effective dose of 72.7 Gy under the linear-quadratic model (LQ) but 59.4 Gy under the linear-quadratic-linear model (LQL).

Free BED and EQD2 calculator: LQ or LQL?
