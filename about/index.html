About

I am a physicist and Research Director at Mines Paris–PSL, within the Centre Observation, Impacts, Énergie (O.I.E.), and Visiting Professor at the University of Kragujevac, Serbia. My work connects solar and photovoltaic forecasting, time series and frugal AI. My background also includes medical physics.

Background

I trained in fundamental physics, qualified as a medical physicist in 2003, and completed my PhD on solar radiation time series in 2011. I pursued energy research alongside clinical work before joining Mines Paris–PSL as Research Director in 2024.

My experience in radiotherapy and measurement informs how I approach uncertainty, model evaluation and scientific software. Read my academic CV.

Current Research

I study solar variability and forecastability, parsimonious time-series models and the evaluation of forecasts against meaningful baselines. Applications include joint energy-demand and generation forecasting, storage and microgrid operation. My medical-physics research addresses dose equivalence and modeling.

Recent first-author papers introduce the sCV and forecastability indices, develop BLEND forecasting, and examine tolerance-based validation with the γ-index.

Research Projects

My current collaborative work includes Fine4Cast (2023–2028), the ELECTRE program, and the Mines Paris–PSL / University of Corsica / CNRS research agreement (2024–2027). These activities connect forecasting, AI and energy management.

Earlier work includes SAPHIR, HyLES, INTELPOLY and the TILOS island demonstrator. Explore the research themes and project portfolio.

Recognition and Service

I serve as Associate Editor of Scientific Reports and on the Sustainability editorial board. I also contribute to research-proposal evaluation (Swiss National Science Foundation, Swiss Cancer Research, Kuwait Foundation for the Advancement of Sciences), doctoral supervision and the ETIP PV working group on Digital PV, Grid and Storage.

Selected distinctions include the PVSEC Award (Hamburg, 2009) and the City of Nice Prize (2012). Appointments, service and career.
