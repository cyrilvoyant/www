About

I am a physicist and Research Director at Mines Paris – PSL, within the Centre Observation, Impacts, Energy (O.I.E.), and Visiting Professor at the University of Kragujevac, Serbia. I build physical and statistical models to inform decisions in energy and medicine, with people at the center and AI as one tool among others. Our work connects solar and photovoltaic forecasting, time series and frugal AI. My background also includes medical physics.

In brief

  • Physicist, Research Director at Mines Paris – PSL since 2024, after medical physics in radiotherapy from 2003 to 2024.
  • Two fields, one way of working: energy forecasting and medical physics, with the model at the core, people at the center and AI as a tool at the edge.
  • All public work is gathered at a glance; the short version: in 30 seconds.

Background

I trained in fundamental physics, qualified as a medical physicist in 2003, and completed my PhD on solar radiation time series in 2011. I pursued energy research alongside clinical work as a medical physicist at Polyclinique Saint-Roch, Montpellier (2003–2006), then the Castelluccio hospital in Ajaccio (2006–2024), before joining Mines Paris – PSL as Research Director in 2024. My appointments at the University of Corsica ended in 2024; since then I have taught there as a part-time lecturer (vacataire).

My experience in radiotherapy and measurement informs how I approach uncertainty, model evaluation and scientific software. Read my academic CV.

Current research

I study solar variability and forecastability, parsimonious time-series models and the evaluation of forecasts against meaningful baselines. Applications include joint energy-demand and generation forecasting, storage and microgrid operation. My medical-physics research addresses dose equivalence and modeling.

Recent papers introduce the sCV and forecastability indices (sCV: variability caused by clouds; F: how forecastable the signal is), develop BLEND forecasting (a better persistence forecast: the last value mixed with the value one cycle earlier), and examine tolerance-based validation with the γ-index (a test that accepts small shifts in space, time and intensity).

Research projects

Our current collaborative work:

  • Fine4Cast (2023–2028): forecasting renewable production and consumption from minutes to days at fine geographic resolution. Funded by the ANR (the French National Research Agency) within PEPR TASE (PEPR: one of the French priority research programs).
  • ELECTRE (since 2025): a program of the Carnot M.I.N.E.S institute, which brings together research teams of the Écoles des Mines; scientific coordination of the parts on AI for energy, forecasting and multi-source systems.
  • Mines Paris – PSL / University of Corsica / CNRS agreement (CNRS: the French National Centre for Scientific Research; 2024–2027): solar forecasting and energy-management algorithms for microgrids (small local electricity networks).

Earlier programs:

  • SAPHIR (ANR project started in January 2022): sensor-augmented weather prediction at high resolution, combining observations and numerical modeling.
  • HyLES (ANR, 2021–2024): integrating hydrogen into weakly interconnected electricity networks.
  • INTELPOLY (COST Action, 2021–2025; COST: European Cooperation in Science and Technology): diagnosis, control and cybersecurity of polygeneration (several forms of energy produced on one site) and hybrid-storage installations.
  • TILOS (Horizon 2020, the EU research framework program; February 2015 – January 2019; Technology Innovation for the Local Scale, Optimum Integration of Battery Energy Storage): a demonstrator on the Greek island of Tilos, combining battery storage and renewable generation. CORDIS record.

Recognition and service

I serve as Editorial Board Member of Scientific Reports and on the Sustainability editorial board. I also contribute to research-proposal evaluation (Swiss National Science Foundation, Swiss Cancer Research, Kuwait Foundation for the Advancement of Sciences), doctoral supervision and the ETIP PV (European Technology and Innovation Platform for Photovoltaics, PV) working group on Digital PV, Grid and Storage.

Selected distinctions include the PVSEC Award (Hamburg, 2009) of the Photovoltaic Solar Energy Conference and the City of Nice Prize (2012). Appointments, service and career.
