My research in 30 seconds

I am a physicist and Research Director at Mines Paris – PSL. I build and evaluate models to help inform decisions in energy and medical physics.

The thread connecting my work

I start from the phenomenon, the human question and the decision to inform. If the relation is known, I compute it directly; if a model is incomplete, I complement it; if a relation is missing, I measure what learning adds against an explicit reference. I add complexity only when its benefit is demonstrated. AI is one tool among others; method, verification and responsibility remain central.

  1. 1 Compute directlyIs the link between causes and effects known?
  2. 2 HybridizeIs the model enough?
  3. 3 Learn frugallyAre the useful relations known?
  4. 4 AI in its placeHow can people use these tools?
  5. 5 Demonstrate trustHow do we know it works?

Steps 1 to 3 depend on what is already known; AI stays a tool (4); everything is checked (5).
